# Town of Harmony — New Website Walkthrough

**Prepared for:** Town of Harmony Municipal Staff
**Date:** May 5, 2026
**Site:** [www.townofharmony.org](https://www.townofharmony.org)

---

## 1. Homepage

Visitors land on a hero image of Tomlinson-Moore Family Park with the town shield and a welcome message. Two buttons sit up top: "Discover Our History" and "Meeting Agendas," which are the two things people ask about most.

**Screenshot:** `01-homepage-hero.png`

Scroll down and there are three more sections:

- **Town Services** grid with cards for Events, Report an Issue, Board of Aldermen, Interactive Map, Business Directory, Resident Resources, and Contact Us. Each card has a one-line description.
- **Upcoming Events** pulls in the next three events automatically. Right now: May Farmers Market (May 8), June Farmers Market (Jun 12), and the HWY 21 3-Day Road Market (Jul 24). "View Full Calendar" links to the full events page.
- **Community Spotlight** features the Harmony Hill Camp Meeting, the two-week revival tradition from 1846 that gave the town its name. Links out to the History page.

**Screenshot:** `01-homepage-full.png`

## 2. Navigation

The top nav bar is on every page. Here's how it breaks down:

A utility bar across the very top shows the phone number (704-546-2339), street address, and quick links to Events and Emergency Info. Below that, the town shield and name, then the main menu: Home, Agenda & Minutes, Explore, Town, and Contact. There's also a search bar (keyboard shortcut: Cmd+K).

Three of those menu items have dropdowns:

| Menu | Links inside |
|------|-------|
| Agenda & Minutes | Town Meetings, Agendas & Minutes |
| Explore | Interactive Map, Points of Interest, Events |
| Town | Our Team, History, Resources, Sewer Services |

**Screenshot:** `02-nav-agenda-dropdown.png`

## 3. Our Team — elected officials and staff

This page lists everyone by role, in three groups:

- **Executive** — Mayor Sean Turner (serving since 12/2023) and Town Clerk & Finance Officer Wanda Edwards. Photo, title, and phone number on each card.
- **Board of Aldermen** — Scotty Harris, Chris Pierce, Brandon Angell, and Jared Clark. Each shows term dates (2024–2026 or 2024–2028) and a direct phone number.
- **Staff** — Farmers Market Manager Doug Galliher and Maintenance's Tim Trivette.

Previously, staff names were buried in PDFs. Now anyone can look up who to call from their phone.

**Screenshot:** `06-our-team-full.png`

## 4. Town Meetings and Minutes

This is where Board of Aldermen meeting schedules and archived minutes live. Each meeting gets a card showing:

- Meeting type (Council), status badge (Completed), date, time (usually 7:00 PM), and location (Town Hall)
- A "Minutes" download link for the official record
- Both regular Board of Aldermen meetings and Special Meetings appear here

Four meetings from July–August 2025 are currently listed. New meetings show up automatically as they're scheduled and completed.

**Screenshot:** `03-meetings-full.png`

## 5. Events and calendar

The Events page has two filter dropdowns at the top: Category (community, recreation, etc.) and Month. Below the filters, event cards show the name, category tags, date, and details. Recurring events get a repeat icon.

Current listings include the monthly Farmers Markets and the annual HWY 21 Road Market.

**Screenshot:** `04-events-viewport.png`

## 6. Contact page

The contact page has three parts:

1. **A contact form** with fields for First Name, Last Name, Email, Phone (optional), Inquiry Type (dropdown), and Message. "Send Message" delivers the inquiry straight to staff.
2. **Town Hall info** — 3389 Harmony Hwy, Harmony, NC 28634. Phone: (704) 546-2339. Office hours: Monday–Friday, 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM.
3. **Emergency info** — dial 911 for life-threatening emergencies; (704) 546-2300 for non-emergency fire department matters.

There's also a Facebook follow link at the bottom for ongoing updates.

**Screenshot:** `05-contact-full.png`

## 7. History of Harmony

A timeline page covering the town's story from 1846 to today. It opens with the basics: Harmony sits at Highway 21 N and Highway 901 in north Iredell County, 2020 census population of 543.

The timeline starts with 1846–1907 (Camp Meeting Grounds Era, when the Harmony Hill Camp Meeting began), then traces incorporation in 1927 and development up to the present.

**Screenshot:** `07-history-viewport.png`

---

## 8. Interactive business map

A map of Harmony with a pin for every local business, color-coded by category. Ten categories: Restaurants & Food, Retail & Shopping, Auto & Transport, Health & Wellness, Banking & Finance, Home & Garden, Professional & Other, Churches & Religious, Gas & Fuel, and Other. Click any to toggle those pins on or off.

There's a search bar to find a specific business by name, and each listing below the map shows the address, phone, website, and tags.

29 businesses listed. The map boundary follows the actual town limits.

**Screenshot:** `08-map-viewport.png`

---

## 9. Mobile experience

The site works on phones and tablets. On a phone screen:

- The header shrinks down to the shield, phone number, and quick links (Events, Emergency Info). A hamburger menu replaces the full nav bar.
- Tapping the hamburger opens a slide-out menu with a search bar at the top, then all navigation sections with sub-pages listed out for one-tap access.
- Forms stack vertically. The contact form fields go single-column, and buttons are sized for thumbs.
- Text resizes so there's no pinch-to-zoom needed.

**Screenshots:** `09-mobile-homepage.png`, `10-mobile-menu-open.png`, `11-mobile-contact.png`

## 10. Footer

Same footer on every page:

- Town name and tagline ("Where Harmony LIVES and SINGS!"), founding year (1927)
- Address, phone, and email (info@townofharmony.org)
